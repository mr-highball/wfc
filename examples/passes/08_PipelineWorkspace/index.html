HOST POLICY / CALLER-ADJUSTABLE ALLOWANCES
Bound work explicitly, not composition length

      
All values are positive Integer allowances. They limit retained logical inventories or encoded outputs, not exact peak memory, elapsed time, or musical duration. Large histories require full replay and may need several limits raised together. Existing versioned format and compiler capacities still apply.
