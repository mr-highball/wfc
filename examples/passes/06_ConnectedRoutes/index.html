HOW TO READ THE LAB

Connectivity lives on the routing pass.

01

Root and terminals

Every required destination must participate and be reachable from the one declared root through reciprocal selected ports.

02

All or required only

All-participants mode rejects every disconnected route island. Turn it off to permit optional islands while the root and terminals remain connected.

03

Selective repair

Changing a bridge or shaft reopens roads/circulation and their descendants. The upstream terrain or structure baseline is reused unchanged.

04

Two-floor projection

The circulation case draws each Z layer as a separate panel. A paired up/down shaft port is the real edge between the two floors.
