COMPOSITION -

01 / Session

Dimensions are yours. Depth includes the ground floor and air ceiling (minimum 3). Larger volumes need more memory and search time.

02 / Search & repair

A repair never changes an earlier layer outside its scope. A changed terrain input needs terrain repair or full generation.

03 / Constrain one cell

Coordinates are zero-based. Ferns need grass below; flowers need a flowerbed. Plants cannot occupy solid terrain.

What is learned—and what is authored?

Observed volumes. Two independent 3 × 3 × 5 samples teach all six cardinal neighbor directions. Their occupied column heights are shown below. Horizontal D4 symmetry preserves gravity.

2 3 4     3 3 2
3 4 3     4 3 3
4 3 2     3 2 2

Explicit constraints. Ground and ceiling are pinned; intermediate cells are not a copied blueprint. Structural sockets and vertical support stay active. Foliage reads both the same cell and the structural cell below it.

Every composition is independently validated before commit. The inset plant blocks are presentation glyphs, not botanical meshes. This is cardinal adjacency learning, not structural engineering or an overlapping-volume model.
